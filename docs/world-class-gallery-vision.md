# Cosmic Signature — the world-class gallery: analysis, vision and plan

*Creative direction, 26 October 2026. Produced by a multi-agent brainstorm (six audit lenses, a benchmark study of 31 reference sites, eight competing concepts, three judges, a synthesis and a completeness pass). Written against the live site (cosmic-signature-art.vercel.app), the 24 audit screenshots, the production code in `src/`, the four design docs and the v2 mockup. Nothing here alters a pixel of any work or a word of any auction rule.*

---

## 1. Honest analysis of today

The short version: the engineering under this site is museum-grade and the hang is not. The code treats the art with real care (native aspect everywhere, decode-before-commit, one playback lease, hash-verified derivatives, a native `<dialog>` with focus return, reduced-motion respected down to the view-transition pseudo-elements). The presentation then puts that care inside a template: warm paper, Cormorant, tracked eyebrows at 7–10 px, hairline rules, arrows on everything, and the work at a quarter of the screen. Nothing on screen is derived from the art, and the one authentic drama the collection owns — a film that finishes by becoming its still, in two materials of one token — is spent as a 65 px thumbnail in a corner card.

### What works and must survive

- **The paper is already the kozo.** Site paper `#f1ebdf` was sampled from the films (docs/media-and-indexer-integration.md §Visual material measurement). My own 8 px border reads of the three curated Ink stills give `#f0eade` (023), `#efe9dd` (031), `#ece6db` (035): one to five units from the wall. On shots 02, 06 and 07 the sheet's edge nearly disappears. This is the seed of the whole identity and nobody has used it yet.
- **Light's field is true black.** All three curated Light stills' 8 px borders measure exactly `#000000` (only 023/031/035 were sampled; the Phase 0 plate-records script asserts this per Light still rather than assuming it). The viewer (`artwork-viewer.module.css .room[data-expression='light'] { background:#000 }`), the room dialog and the process page's night chapter already put Light on `#000`, and docs/screenshots/immersive-light.png (one work, black, the words Ink and Light at 24 px) is the best screen the site has.
- **The media discipline.** `collection-motion.ts` grants one playback lease with centre weighting and 0.18 hysteresis; `artwork-stage.tsx` decodes the incoming still before React commits an expression change; `artwork-room.tsx` keeps the complete film out of the DOM until chosen and detaches `src` on close — for the three curated works that complete film is the hash-verified 1280×828 local delivery (8.06, 15.74 and 8.35 MB) that `preferVerifiedLocalDelivery` (`media.ts:118–140`) substitutes for the 96 MB original, whose URL stays attached as `sourceUrl`; `media-policy.ts` honours Save-Data and 2g/3g; `.art-media { aspect-ratio: 3456/2234 }` means CLS is clean on every page.
- **Authored crossings exist.** `artwork-stage.module.css` already encodes material in direction: `revealLight` wipes in from the left (`inset(0 100% 0 0)` → `inset(0)`), `revealInk` from the right, 650 ms, `cubic-bezier(0.22,1,0.36,1)`. `process.module.css` proves the whole header can flip to black via `:has(.page)`.
- **The honesty copy.** "The displayed films are recorded renderings of the artwork, not live simulations running in this browser"; "A transfer may represent custody rather than a sale"; the six auction rules with plain titles ("7 days to look", "No administrator overrides"). This is the house voice.
- **The headlines.** "The shape of motion." (already the document title, `layout.tsx:15`), "Physics, upon physics.", "First, an orbit. / Then, a current.", "Every motion has an echo.", "This work is not here." are keepers; §4 says where each one lives.
- **The assets are already there.** 71 verified 640×414 Ink excerpts in `public/art/previews` (8.0 s each, 110–937 KB, 37.7 MB / 37,652,880 bytes in all), 640 `web_preview` / `ember_web_preview` stills in every manifest (12–27 KB), faststart curated films (`moov` at byte 36), the spectral sweep and 64 bins advertised per seed, 16-bit masters, and per-token metadata carrying dominant wavelength, body hues, Borda rank, masses, fate and braid. The collection is 72 observed works (000–071); 71 have published Ink films, and token 000 has Light only (docs/media-and-indexer-integration.md:59).

### What holds it back

**Every entrance opens on an empty wall (critical).** Shots 01, 10, 21 and 23 are paper and type; shot 23 is a phone stage still blank after six seconds with the transport reading `00:00 / 00:00`. The technical cause is a chain of six decisions:

1. `artwork-media.tsx` renders one `<Image src={media.still} width={3456} unoptimized priority>`. `unoptimized` means no `srcset`, no `sizes`, no placeholder; the browser's only candidate is the 325–515 KB full still. `priority` does emit a head preload for it (Next 16's `get-img-props.js:480` sets `preload: preload || priority`, and `image-component.js:221–231` calls `ReactDOM.preload`), so the only image preload on the page is for the heaviest file; nothing preloads the 640 preview and nothing preconnects to a1.
2. `catalog.ts` builds curated works with `thumbnail: entry.still`, and `preferVerifiedLocalDelivery` overwrites a live work's 16 KB thumbnail with that same full still, so Signatures 023/031/035 — the hero, Selected Works, the Atlas, the auction feature — always load the heaviest file. `curated-media.json` has no thumbnail entries at all.
3. `.art-media { background:#f1ebdf }` is the page paper, so the reserved box is invisible while it waits (shot 01 shows a 70 %-wide void you cannot see).
4. `MotionProvider`'s server snapshot is `() => true`, so SSR HTML carries `.motion-paused` and no `<video>`; the still is discovered early by the preload, but the hero's 8 MB film cannot start fetching until JS downloads, hydrates, the IntersectionObserver fires and `onLoadedMetadata` seeks to `startAt=9`. Shot 02's clock reads 00:12: the film became visible about three seconds after it began at second 9.
5. `layout.tsx` emits no `<link rel="preconnect">` to a1.cosmicsignature.com; the 68 non-curated works pay DNS + TLS only when the `<img>` or the preload is discovered. `next.config.ts` applies immutable caching only to `/art/previews/*`, so hero stills revalidate on every return visit.
6. `art/[id]/page.tsx` awaits `getArtwork()` (a record read plus a manifest read, each bounded at 5 s) before rendering anything; `loading.tsx` replaces `<main>` with a 45 px `∴` whose `@keyframes orbit` ends at 332°, so it visibly jumps every 8 s. Then the viewer mounts an empty 919 px plate, then the still pops in with no transition (`.art-media img` has none; only `video` has `opacity 0.7s`).

The fix is also the design: ship the publisher's own 640 preview inline (a data URI for the route's one hero, preloaded for the rest) painted at full plate size on the work's own ground at 0 ms; settle the full still over it on `decode()`; server-render the hero `<video poster muted playsinline preload="metadata">` so the preload scanner opens the connection before hydration while autoplay stays a JS decision; preconnect; one `fetchPriority="high"` image per page; a custom `next/image` loader that only maps widths to published hashed derivatives; replace `loading.tsx` with the destination's own skeleton so the shared-element morph lands on an image. No pixel is invented.

**The art is hung small; nowhere does a work own the wall (critical).** Home: `.passage-field` is 70 % wide, pinned `top:42px; right:2.3%`, and because 023's composition has wide empty kozo margins the visible ink is roughly a quarter of the viewport (shot 02). Artwork page: `.plate { width: min(100%, calc(max(260px, 66svh) * 1.547)) }` = 919 px at 1440×900 (26.6 % of native), under an 82 px topbar and above a 60 px transport (shot 11). The Light echo is capped at `max-width:1000px` (shot 15). The process Light film sits in a `0.72fr 1.8fr` grid beside copy (shot 16); the Ink plate is `width:88%` (shot 18). The room dialog keeps header, controls and notice rows on screen even in `:fullscreen`. The work is never shown above about 32 % of native anywhere on the site (the room dialog, the largest, is ≈ 1109 px wide), so the 1–2 px Light filaments and the kozo grain inside 031's vortices are never visible.

**The identity is generic luxury-minimal; nothing in the UI comes from the art (critical).** Four two-line Cormorant headlines share one shape, three with an italic second line ("The shape / of motion.", "Follow what / moves you.", "Eight works. / One continuing story.") and one all roman ("One identity. / Many expressions.", shot 18). Numbered eyebrows `01 / 02` appear on the hero kicker, the collection tabs, the viewer tabs and the process chapters. `ArrowUpRight` is attached to nearly every link at seven different pixel sizes (12–20), and `Auctions ↗` in the main nav uses the external-link glyph for an internal route. A 500 px `8` at 27 % opacity floats behind the auction invitation (shot 04). Clash Display (wordmark) + Cormorant + Inter are three unrelated voices; the footer uses a fourth setting (Cormorant "Cosmic Signature", shot 04). The brand mark is a 30 px circle with three dots on a 35 s rotation — a diagram of a tidy orbit, which is the one thing the art refutes.

**The 7–10 px caption world is a tic, not prestige (major).** I counted 108 `font-size` declarations in px at or below 11 px across `globals.css` and the module files (1 at 6 px, 3 at 7, 16 at 8, 22 at 9, 30 at 10, 36 at 11) on a 14 px root, plus five rem sizes in `artwork-room.module.css` (0.6–0.75 rem = 8.4–10.5 px) that a 12 px floor must also catch. The hero record is 8 px uppercase over a 1 px bar over 9 px actions; atlas captions are 7 px (6 px under 480 px); the viewer clock is 9 px; body copy is 12–13 px Inter in 320–350 px columns. Several sizes get smaller on phones. CJK locales inherit the Latin `h1` tracking (`-0.045em` to `-0.055em`) and `line-height: 0.85` tuned for Cormorant, with `--serif` falling to whatever Mincho/Songti the OS has (`globals.css` 120–130); nothing CJK is self-hosted.

**Light is a basement, not a second gallery (major).** On the home page Light is reachable only through `.material-portal`, a 230 px black card with a 65 px thumbnail and a 7 px eyebrow (shot 02); on a phone it is 700 px below the plate it changes (shot 22). The crossing is `.material-curtain`: a `position:fixed; inset:0` sheet that always enters from the left (`@keyframes crossing`, `translateX(-102%)` → 0) over 1.25 s regardless of direction, covers the header, and lands on a motionless still. Four different crossing grammars coexist: the curtain, the plate-only `revealLight/revealInk`, the static three-dot `.materialSeam` on the process page (shot 17), and the room's 600 ms background ease.

**The resolution is never staged (major).** Both published films end on their stills (docs/art-presentation-research.md), yet `passage.tsx` starts the hero at `startAt={9}` so no visitor ever sees blank kozo receive its first mark, and `artwork-media.tsx` holds 1.6 s on `ended`, fades 600 ms and rewinds to 0 — through roughly six seconds of near-empty paper on every loop. The 71 catalogue excerpts are cut 6–14 s (`collection-previews.json startSeconds: 6`) so each one ends on a jump cut dressed as a 700 ms fade; every artwork page with an excerpt — all 71, the curated three included, because `artwork-viewer.tsx:65–71` sets `films: [media.previewFilm]` — plays that same 640 excerpt as its inline hero film, upscaled 1.4× into the 919 px plate and labelled "Web preview" in the transport, while the 1280 curated films are reserved for the room. **The collection is a contact sheet and the Atlas opens empty (major).** `/collection` stacks eyebrow, a 109 px h1 (`clamp(66px, 7.6vw, 124px)` at 1440), description, `01/02` tabs, a note, an h2 and a search form before the Suspense boundary; at 1440×900 the first row of stills is the bottom 30 % (shot 05), on a phone no work is in the viewport (shot 24). **The record is a protocol spec sheet; there is no author (major).** `art/[id]/page.tsx` prints "Signature 31" at 76 px, then `t.workCopy` — the same sentence on all 71 works and on every auction lot — then "The life of a work." at 86 px (`clamp(56px, 6vw, 90px)` at 1440), then a `<dl>` of full 42-character hex in 11 px monospace (shot 12), then the traits as a three-column table in a beige `#e9e2d5` panel (shot 13). Roughly twenty durations and six curves are in use. No entrance, no scroll choreography at the material seams, no type motion except the 10 px caption inside the curtain. **Mobile stacks the desktop (critical on phones).** At ≤850 px the header wraps to two rows and two rules, 156 CSS px before any content (shots 21–24); on `/art/23` the breadcrumb row pushes the stage to ~330 px. **Fidelity (major).** All 71 artwork pages play the 640×414, ~107 kbps excerpt as the inline film; all ambient films are 30 fps while the source is 60. The bid panel itself (`live-auction.tsx`) is correctly calm and exact — it already renders refund and withdraw (`:523–543`) — and must not change.

## 2. The bar

People fall off chairs for art online in a handful of repeatable ways, and none of them is interface decoration.

- **Depth as proof.** The Rijksmuseum's 717-gigapixel *Night Watch* and the Mauritshuis's 10-gigapixel *Girl*: black surround, three glyphs of chrome, and four zoom steps from the whole canvas to cracks in paint. Google Art Camera opens *inside* a detail and pulls back. Cosmic Signature ships 3456×2234 stills and 16-bit masters and shows them at 26 %.
- **Truth beneath the surface.** *Inside Bruegel* (KHM) switches one panel between visible, infrared and X-ray in register. Light, Ink, the 10 s spectral sweep and 64 wavelength bins share one frame; no generative platform has that.
- **Several truthful orderings of one archive.** Es Devlin's List / Spectrum / Timeline; Feral File's filters; Reas's index by medium; Hobbs's constellation of ~16 works and the word "Menu". Every Signature carries a dominant wavelength in nanometres, a cycle and a mint date.
- **Exactness as luxury.** Art Blocks' honest "LIVE FROM" eyebrow; QQL's hash captions; a provenance line stamped with the block time at which it was read. `provenance.checkedOn` already exists in `types.ts`.
- **The threshold ritual.** The National Gallery's *Imaginarium*: Start → sound opt-in → narrated intro with Skip → Enter → persistent Mute. Sound is never autoplayed.
- **Exhibition as event with a persistent record.** Bright Moments choreographed every reveal and kept the archive; Feral File exhibitions open at a UTC time and never close. Performance Cycles already have this structure.
- **Performance as aesthetic.** Lusion v3 and Igloo Inc won Awwwards years with a 60 fps first frame on phones. A gasp cannot happen on an empty sheet.
- **Chrome reduced to almost nothing on art screens; material, not theme.** Feral File lives on black, Le Random on paper; neither offers a toggle. Hauser & Wirth and Hobbs hide navigation on arrival.
- **An author's voice.** Reas's essay, Sougwen's §-indexed statement, Louisiana Channel's pull quotes. One first-person paragraph changes the register from protocol to artist.

**Patterns to avoid**, each of which this site currently brushes against: the 2026 quiet-luxury template (beige, Cormorant, 8–10 px tracked eyebrows); blank stages and blur-up placeholders for art; virtual 3D rooms, paper curls and page flips; cursor-following liquid or refraction near the plate (it would alter NFT pixels and read as a WebGL demo); autoplaying sound, percentage preloaders, click-and-hold gates; marketplace chrome in the gallery layer (prices on cards, "Collect" in the hero, a search bar at the top); infinite thumbnail clouds; micro type as prestige; faking liveness (scroll-scrubbed frames presented as the finished work, Light/Ink as a theme toggle); decorative ambient motion that is not the art (the 22 s drift); smooth-scroll libraries and bot walls on the front door.

## 3. The vision: **Two Grounds**

**Thesis.** Every Cosmic Signature was authored with its own wall built in. The Ink sheet's kozo *is* the paper; the Light field's black *is* the dark room. So the venue is made of exactly two grounds, both taken from the work, and nothing else: a paper wing where each Ink plate is printed edge-to-edge on a wall painted to that sheet's own tone, and a night wing where each Light plate floats on `#000` with no edge at all. The visitor never sees a frame. The only things that move are the work's own film, a plate coming to rest, and the one gesture the venue owns: the Turn, a straight seam that carries wall and work together from paper to night and back. Around the plates sits the apparatus of a catalogue raisonné (number, entry, cycle, provenance, files, colophon) composed with the exactness collectors trust. Ink is the first encounter on every route, as the decision register records, with one stated exception: a work without a published Ink expression (token 000 today) opens in Light, its label says so, and no Turn is offered. Light is one Turn away at identical scale on every screen, never a theme of the other.

This is Concept 2's architecture (the plate fitted by height, the running head and Index sheet, the 1.547-derived scale with a 12 px floor, the entry with footnoted glossary, chapters by cycle, the sale as a bound catalogue, the colophon) with its book props removed, Concept 6's Turn and Index of numbers grafted in as the everyday crossing and the collection's index, Concept 4's Dusk as the home's one automatic event, its eye-line hang and its `Sit`, Concept 1's timings and chrome-less room, Concept 5's record layer and Verify-a-download, Concept 7's two-clocks law and theatre pause, and Concept 8's Spectrum ordering and "next star".

### Identity

**Material logic.** Two stocks and one ink. *Paper*: the page ground stays `#f1ebdf`, but every Ink plate is letterboxed in a per-work tone sampled offline from its own still's 8 px border and stored in `src/lib/art/plate-records.json` beside its still hash (`#f0eade` for 023, `#efe9dd` for 031, `#ece6db` for 035; a single constant would show a faint rectangle on 035). Because ink reaches the border on all three curated works (minimum border luminance 62, 72 and 20 of 255), the record also carries an *edge flag*: a work whose ink touches the edge is hung with a deliberate 1 px hairline rather than an accidental one. *Night*: `#000000`, continuous with the Light field and asserted per work by the same script; whenever a Light plate dominates the viewport, the whole viewport is black — never a black tile on cream (today's `.passage.material-light` paints `#080a09` around a `#000` artwork, so the sheet's edge shows and drifts). Type is `#22231f` on paper and `#f1ebdf` on night; rules are 1 px at 24 % of the type colour; the only colour on the site is in the Light plates. The current ground is carried on `<html data-room="paper|night">`, set by route and by an IntersectionObserver at each seam, driving tokens, the running head, `theme-color` and (with `viewportFit:'cover'`) the phone's status bar. This generalises the `:has(.page)` header flip already in `process.module.css`.

**Typography.** One book face and one instrument face, both OFL, so the identity never waits on a licence:

- **Literata** (TypeTogether; variable opsz 7–72, wght 200–900, roman and italic; Latin extended, Cyrillic for uk, Vietnamese; true small caps `smcp/c2sc`, old-style `onum` and tabular lining `tnum`) for display, reading, labels and the wordmark. Self-hosted via `next/font/local`, display subset with `font-display: optional` so a headline never swaps in front of the art.
- **IBM Plex Mono** (OFL; Latin, Cyrillic and Vietnamese sets published by IBM, with `№` U+2116 in its Cyrillic set) at 12 px for readouts and 15 px for controls, tabular lining figures throughout, for everything the instrument says: `№ 031`, clocks, nanometres, hashes, addresses, lot states, scale readouts. Chosen over Geist Mono because its coverage of `№`, `·`, Cyrillic and Vietnamese is documented; the CI glyph check in Phase 1 asserts it anyway.
- **CJK display subsets per locale** via `unicode-range`: Noto Serif SC / TC / HK, Shippori Mincho B1 (ja), Gowun Batang (ko), cut by a build step (`scripts/build-font-subsets.mjs`, pyftsubset or glyphhanger over all eight locale files, the statement, the glossary and the generated entry strings), with `unicode-range` falling through to the system face for anything outside and a CI check that fails when a string uses a glyph the subset lacks. Per-`:lang()` tokens: letter-spacing 0, display line-height 1.25, reading 16–17 px / 1.9, labels 12 px regular, no uppercase, no small caps. The work identifier, clocks and hashes stay in Plex Mono in every locale so `№ 031` is identical in Kyiv and Taipei.
- Cormorant Garamond, Clash Display and Inter are retired.

**Scale.** Ratio 1.547 (the plate's own proportion, 3456/2234), applied every two steps (1.2438 per step), fluid with `clamp()` between 375 and 1440 px, on a 9 px baseline grid:

| Stop | px | Use |
|---|---|---|
| folio | 12 | captions, small caps, mono readouts; **the floor in every locale** |
| control | 15 | every control and link word (`Again`, `Sit`, `Light`, `Study`, `Collection →`), Index numerals, marginalia, glossary footnotes |
| reading | 18 / 27 | entry, statement, conditions of sale; measure 34–38 em |
| subtitle | 28 | italic subtitle (Fate) |
| plate title | 44 | the hanging numeral beside a plate |
| chapter | 68 | cycle openers, "Then, a current.", the selection line |
| entry | 106 | the numeral at the head of an entry; the statement's title |
| cover | 164 | one line on the one screen that has no work (the sale title) |

The one size outside the scale is 16 px, used only as the iOS floor for text inputs so Safari never zooms. Old-style figures in prose; tabular lining for every identifier, clock and nm value. `hanging-punctuation: first last` and `text-wrap: balance` on display lines; a −0.05 em optical indent on lines beginning T/A/V/W (shot 21's T crossbar is visibly indented today).

**Numbering, one grammar.** `№ 031` is the work's name everywhere (running head, label strip, Index, OG card, alt text, document title). "Signature 031" appears only as the first line of the entry. The bare `031` is the folio in the Index. The five competing forms today ("Signature 023", "№ 031", "Signature 31", "023 INK / FILM", "Signature 031 INK") are retired across all eight locales.

**Navigation, one vocabulary.** Collection · Auctions · Process · Statement are the four words, in the running head, the Index sheet, the footer and all eight locale files; `Index` opens the sheet. The collection's running head reads `Collection · 071–054`; "plate" is the word for the object in copy, never a route name. On phones the Index sheet lists Auctions first so a collector never hunts.

**The wordmark.** Typeset, not drawn: `COSMIC SIGNATURE` in Literata true small caps, 12 px tracked 0.12 em in the running head, 106 px on the statement page; header and footer use the same setting. The rotating three-dot ring, the `∴` spinner and the Clash caps are retired. The only device is a static three-dot mark, derived once from one fixed seed — Signature 031, the work the process essay already uses, with published masses 130.7 : 251.0 : 125.0 — and used as favicon, apple-icon and the colophon's end mark. Static files, a one-time derivation, regenerated only if the owner chooses another seed; it never spins and never changes per cycle. Its accessible name states the thesis in eight locales: "Three bodies, one orbit — Cosmic Signature".

**Chrome on art screens.** A 48 px running head (wordmark left; Collection · Auctions · Process · Statement · Index · EN right; 56 px on phones with only `Index` right), a 56 px label strip beneath the plate (the 44 px hanging numeral sits in it with 6 px of leading above and below), and a 1 px time line at the viewport's bottom edge while a film plays. That is all. `EN` is a Popover-API menu with 44 px rows that closes on outside tap and Escape and preserves `?view=`, `?q=` and `?offset=`. No breadcrumb row, no numbered eyebrows outside the essay's chapters, no watermark numeral, no pill controls, no hover lifts, no drift. `↗` means one thing: this link leaves cosmic.art. Internal links use the square 1 px underline that thickens to 2 px for 120 ms on hover and `:active`. Keyboard: Space, ← →, I/L, F, Z, `/`, digits-then-Enter, `?` — all suppressed while focus is in an input, textarea, contenteditable or the bid sheet, and all listed in the `?` sheet.

### Motion vocabulary

Named moments:

1. **Focus.** The real 640 preview is present at 0 ms; the full still fades over it on `decode()`, `--t-focus`, `--ease-settle`, opacity only, never scale (scaling moves pixels). The still is then held as an object for `--t-hold` before any film may start — one token on every plate, home included; the home's gasp is motion arriving on a still the eye has already registered, not motion replacing a blank. The film gains its first decoded frame over `--t-reel` (the existing `has-frame` rule). The same beats on every plate on every page; this is also the loading state.
2. **The Turn.** 80 ms of nothing (the room takes a breath), then one straight vertical seam travels the whole viewport over `--t-turn` with `--ease-turn`: the night layer (black ground + Light still + Light film slot, already decoded) is revealed with `clip-path: inset(0 0 0 X%)`, X 100 → 0. Light always arrives from the right; Ink always returns from the left. This deliberately flips `revealLight`/`revealInk` (which wipe Light in from the left and Ink from the right today) and the curtain (which always enters from the left): the gatefold places Ink on the left leaf and Light on the right, so on every page the seam moves the way the book opens, and one direction rule replaces both. The running head and label are in both layers, so they invert as the seam crosses them. Hold-to-peek: hold `L` from Ink or `I` from Light and the seam returns on release; a tap of the same key toggles. On touch the seam follows the finger across the plate and completes past 40 %; a drag that does not pass 40 % springs back. One grammar replaces four.
3. **Resolution.** On `ended`: the time line reaches 100 % and freezes; the live dot stops; the label flips `film 00:30` → `still`; nothing else on the page moves for `--t-rest` (8 s; 6 s on the home, `--t-rest-dusk`, because the Dusk follows); then one word, `Again`, at 15 px. Replay is forward-only from frame 0. No automatic loop.
4. **Sit.** A 15 px word in the label strip of every plate with a film (home screen 1, artwork Beat 1, process chapters, lot plates). Pressing it fades all chrome — head, strip, time line — over `--t-sit`, lets the film play to its still and rest, and offers `Again` after `--t-rest`; any input returns the chrome at `--t-chrome`. One string in eight locales.
5. **The Dusk** (home only, once per visit). After Resolution and its 6 s rest, the paper wall darkens to black over `--t-dusk`; the sheet begins to fade at 35 % of that travel (so its edge is visible for about a second, as an object, while the label reads "The light goes down"); the same token's Light still rises in the same frame at the same eye line. The dawn reverses in `--t-dawn`. Mechanically, screens 1–2 of the home are one 200svh section with a sticky 100svh stage and one custom property `--night` (0 → 1) that drives the night layer's clip and the ground; the scroll sets it through `animation-timeline: view()`, and the Dusk animates the same property by time, then performs an instant `scrollTo` to the matching offset so hand and clock never fight. It is cancelled by any scroll or wheel, any key, `touchstart` anywhere, pointer movement over the label, focus moving within the page, or `visibilitychange` to hidden (cancel, never resume mid-travel). It never fires under reduced motion or Pause, while a `<video>` is fullscreen, with the Index sheet or a bid sheet open, and never on artwork, lot or process pages, where the same crossing is driven only by the visitor's scroll (hard-edge `@supports` fallback).
6. **The Walk.** Card → plate keeps the React `ViewTransition` share (`--t-walk`) but lands on the same 640 bytes the card painted; the label strip and running head enter `--t-chrome` later so the work arrives first and the room assembles around it. Back morphs the work into its card.
7. **The Expansion.** The page plate and the room share one view-transition name during open/close (`--t-expand`), so the work grows in place while the backdrop fades beneath it and the edge strip enters `--t-chrome` later. Close reverses.
8. **One projector.** A single site-wide playback lease generalised from `collection-motion.ts` (eye-line weighted, hysteresis, velocity-aware intent); the outgoing player keeps its frame for `--t-reel` before unmount so losing the lease is a fade, never a cut; nothing autoplays on constrained connections.
9. **Sheets** (Index, label drawer, bid sheet): 24 px rise + opacity over `--t-sheet`.

Type moves at three authored points and nowhere else, as blocks (CJK-safe): the home's one line after Focus; the entry numeral at Resolution; the essay's chapter numerals with the scroll seam.

**Remembered expression.** The chosen expression travels in the URL (`?view=light`, as the pager and the Walk already carry it) and is remembered for the session in `sessionStorage` beside `cosmic-art-motion-v1`; a new visit always opens on Ink; the clock never decides.

**Reduced motion and Pause** are two tiers under one header control with a constant label ("Pause ambient motion") and `aria-pressed`, replacing today's label that flips while the state toggles (`site-header.tsx:54–56`). Ambient off: every beat above is a state — the still is present, the Turn is one frame, the scroll-driven seams are a hard material edge at the section boundary, the Dusk is a plain in-page link to the night wing, chrome is always visible, no type entrance. Films answer only to an explicit `Play` and, once asked for, always play to their still and hold. **Hush** (transaction review, §4 Auctions) is a third state with the same rules: the Turn stays available but instant, scroll seams render as hard edges, films hold on their stills, the lease is released; nothing is disabled, so a collector can still check the other expression before bidding.

### The five moments that get disproportionate craft

These are the docs' five (arrival, passage, reveal, affinity, expansion), now named and specified: **the Arrival** (Focus from inline preview to still to film, with the label narrating truthfully: `arriving → still → film 00:01 → still`); **the Turn** (one seam, three drivers: key/button, finger, scroll); **the Resolution** (the film finishing is the collection's one native event, staged everywhere a full film plays, offered quietly through `Sit`, and never spent on a jump cut); **the Gathering** (the Index of numbers with the work behind the type, and the Spectrum ordering: the collection re-forms around a measurement, not an affinity score); **the Expansion** (plate → chrome-less room → 1:1 study, the work growing without losing context).

## 7. What we will NOT do, and why

- **A cursor-reactive fluid wall** (the "fourth body"): a simulated fluid beside a recorded Navier–Stokes sumi is a second figure on the art's ground, contradicts "recorded renderings, not live simulations", and vanishes for reduced-motion and Save-Data visitors, so it cannot be the identity.
- **Hours by the visitor's sun, hourly UTC screenings, a programme ticker, a brand clock.** A time-driven theme of one expression over the other; it reverses Ink-first for every evening visit, moves the room under a reader, withholds the gasp 59 minutes in 60, and would stampede a single nginx origin at :00. The chosen expression is a remembered choice for the session, never the sun's.
- **"After hours" charcoal walls under `prefers-color-scheme: dark` or a toggle.** A dark-mode toggle in disguise; it exposes every Ink sheet's edge that the edgeless plate exists to hide.
- **No home page, no header on art screens, mandatory `scroll-snap-stop: always` over 72 plates, a corridor driven by the vertical wheel.** Orientation and SEO gambled for purity; scroll-jacking by stylesheet; a collector must see a route to Auctions and the language switch without hunting.
- **House lights down to black before the film.** Taking the art away to show it again reads as a broken image on a slow link; the held still and the film-from-frame-0 beat survive without it.
- **Timed page moves on artwork, lot and process pages.** Only the home plate's film → rest → Dusk is automatic, once, cancelled by any input.
- **Two playback leases on wide desktops.** docs/site-and-collector-experience.md:20 is explicit: one deliberately chosen moving work; a second competes with it, and the gatefold's dual-film mode is the only sanctioned pair, after frame correspondence is verified.
- **A translucent masthead over the plate.** A tinted strip of artwork is a tint; the masthead is opaque in the ground tone or the plate scrolls under it.
- **Book and museum props**: tissue interleaves, recto/verso vocabulary (it makes Light the back of the page), the gatefold's first-time unfolding animation, the asterism as a logo pun, vinyl lettering, a vitrine with a felt floor, a bench, a floor plan.
- **Plate scale from Chaos or `orbit_info.coverage`.** `orbit_info` is null on token 31; and bigger reads as more important, an implied rarity claim the docs forbid. Rhythm is editorial.
- **A pannable thumbnail cloud** (the Orbit view, a free pan-zoom Sky of 62 glints, a 24 × 7 grid of 96 px previews). The Spectrum wall is the honest version.
- **Sonification synthesised from metadata** (wavelength folded 40 octaves, hues as intervals). Numerology attached to the token; sound is a credited commission or nothing.
- **Scroll-scrubbing the film through 120 AVIF frames.** A scrubbed frame is not the finished work; ~4 MB per work for a gesture that fights native scroll.
- **The seed seal pressed after every resolution, the contract address as the wordmark, a wavelength accent on the chrome, the braid drawn before the generator confirms its crossing convention.** A tic by the third visit; a marketplace aesthetic; a design device read as the palette; a false diagram.
- **Living marks** (a real-time three-body integration in the masthead, an orbit trace that needs an export that does not exist, a mark that changes per cycle). The mark is one static derivation from one seed.
- **Buying commercial display faces first** (Signifier, GT Alpina, Söhne, Spectral, Gambetta). Unverified Cyrillic/Vietnamese coverage and licence cost across eight locales; the OFL system is proven first.
- **Claiming frame sync or Display P3 before verification.** The research doc warns Ink films trim pre-roll and the derivatives are untagged.
- **Using the 8 s 640 excerpts as any plate's Film.** They end mid-fluid; they are hover, focus and press previews on desktop and the phone's ambient motion, always labelled `excerpt 00:06–00:14` and always ending on a held frame that fades to the still, until the settling clips exist. They are never what `Film`, `Play` or `Sit` plays.
- **Site-authored sale dates, a Kyiv clock, or any countdown outside the bid panel.** Dates come from the chain and are shown in UTC and the visitor's zone; the only `hh:mm:ss` is the contract's, rendered by `live-auction.tsx`.
- **A custom swipe-back gesture on phones.** It collides with drag-to-Turn; the OS back gesture and `Collection` in the Index sheet are the way home.
- **Pull-down-past-the-top gestures, opt-in tilt, a ledger table as the default collection view, typing digits as the only search.**

## 8. Open questions for the owner

1. **Who writes the statement, and in whose name?** The first-person preface (home, `/statement`, the essay byline, the sale foreword) is the single biggest change of register, and it needs an author: the artist, the studio, or a named curator. The entry and glossary can ship without it; the home's one line cannot.
2. **The Dusk.** Do you accept one automatic event per visit on the home (film → rest → the wall going down to the same orbit in light), cancelled by any input and never on other pages? Two judges want it; one would make even that gesture-only. It ships behind a flag either way.
3. **Ink first, confirmed?** The whole venue opens on paper and reaches Light by one Turn; the one exception is a work with no Ink expression (token 000), which opens in Light and says so. If you ever want Light to lead, the architecture mirrors without changing a beat, but the registry, the OG cards and the statement line are written for Ink-first.
4. **Typeface budget.** We ship Literata + IBM Plex Mono + Noto/Shippori/Gowun subsets (all OFL), with a CI glyph check across all eight locales. Is there a budget and appetite to trial a commercial book face later, or is the free system the decision?
5. **Sound.** Commission a composer (or record the artist's spoken statement) for an opt-in screening room, or keep the venue silent? Nothing audible ships without a credited author.
6. **Pipeline asks.** The generator team is needed for: enumerating the 64 spectral bins with filenames and hashes in `assets.json`; confirming whether the masters' Display P3 `cHRM` is intentional; publishing 1280/1920 stills and (ideally) the settling clips as advertised roles so the site repo stops authoring derivatives; and the frame-correspondence metadata for synchronised Light/Ink playback.
7. **Hosting.** a1.cosmicsignature.com serves everything with `max-age=3600` from one plain nginx origin. The 1:1 tiles, settling clips, the ~3 GB master pull and any opening-night traffic want a CDN and immutable, hash-named URLs. Is that in scope, and when does cosmic.art DNS land (until then `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` must be set on Vercel)?
8. **Naming and counting.** "Sale 01 — Eight New Works · Performance Cycle N" for the sale, "Cycle 1 (August 2026)" instead of "Performance Cycle 1" in labels, with per-cycle work counts read from the index rather than assumed to be eight, and a named curator for "A hang: 023, 031, 035" and the four trails. Please confirm or supply the names; the eight locales follow.
