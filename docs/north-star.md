# North star

Read this before building anything for cosmicsignature.art. Every change answers to it. When a
plan, a design or a pull request does not move the venue toward what is written here, it waits.

The owner set these goals in October and November 2026. They are quoted where the words are the
owner's own.

## The goal

**The best and most amazing website on the internet.** "Not just astonishing… literally the best
and most amazing website on the internet." "The best web designers in the world need to be
absolutely blown away by this site… better than whatever the best web designers can create."

It is three places at once, in this order of importance:

1. **A gallery that astonishes.** A first-time visitor arrives knowing nothing, and the site is
   self-contained: nothing needs explaining before it works. "When people visit it for the first
   time… people need to fall out of their chairs when they see the site."
2. **A science museum.** The site teaches: what the three-body problem is, its history, why it is
   a problem at all and why it cannot be solved in general; and, for the Ink expression, the
   Navier–Stokes equations that carry the ink. Every lesson is the work's own physics, shown
   live, never a diagram of something else.
3. **A place where people want to collect, and to tell others.** Visitors should be impressed
   enough to want to buy a work, and at least to spread the word. The site presents, honestly, how
   exciting and unique the project is: "There's nothing like it out there."

It must also teach how such a site is built: "so well-made that it could be used as a teaching
tool to teach students how to make incredible websites", with "the best web design and web
development principles… best practices everywhere", and a codebase that is world-class too.

## What is unique, and must be said well

- **Born at the end of a cycle.** Nothing here is pre-generated. Each successful round of the
  Cosmic Signature game ends by minting new works on Arbitrum, and their seeds are fixed in that one
  transaction. Until a cycle closes, its works do not exist anywhere, for anyone.
- **Each work is a real physical event.** One orbit of three bodies under gravity, chosen from its
  seed and integrated step by step. Any visitor's browser can derive it again from the seed and
  check it against the published record, to the last bit.
- **Chaos makes each work irreproducible.** Start the same orbit one part in a trillion apart and,
  carried on past the recorded window, the two futures part: the gap doubles again and again until
  they share nothing. Only the exact seed gives the exact work.
- **One work, two expressions of equal standing.** Light: luminous ribbons on black. Ink: the same
  motion driving solid bodies through a two-dimensional Navier–Stokes fluid that carries sumi over
  kozo.
- **A living collection.** It grows by chapters, one per cycle, and each chapter stays as it was
  hung.

## What "done" means

Perfection is never declared; it is measured. A release is done when all of these hold:

- **Every open pull request is merged and live**: nothing built waits off the site.
- **An independent review scores 9 or more in every category** (design, usability, creativity,
  content, mobile, developer), with no gap left open from the review before it. The first
  award-juror review, on 8 November 2026, scored 7.6, 6.9, 8.7, 8.8, 7.3 and 8.3.
- **Real people and real devices agree**:
  - real iPhones, iPads and Android phones;
  - VoiceOver and NVDA;
  - field Core Web Vitals in the good range;
  - designers and first-time visitors who are not the people who built it.
- **The science is right**: every claim on the site has a source a specialist would accept.
- **Visitors act**: more first visits reach a work page, read the science, share a work, and bid.
  Measure these honestly, with consent, before and after each change.

## The rules every change keeps

These are the venue's standing rules. A change that breaks one is not done, however good it looks.

- **The art is never altered.** Original proportions, colours and pixels; nothing over the work;
  derived views are labelled "Analysis, not the work" and never appear on sale surfaces.
- **Motion comes from the art.** Every animation is the work's own physics, order or time; nothing
  decorative; no scroll-jacking.
- **Light and Ink are equal.** Neither is the default in spirit; the day takes turns.
- **Everyone is invited.** Reduced-motion parity, 44 px targets, a 12 px type floor, eight
  languages with key parity, keyboard and screen-reader paths, no horizontal overflow at 320 px.
- **Honesty sells.** Real scarcity, real deadlines and real activity only: never fake urgency,
  fake scarcity, fake social proof or pressure, and never a promise or hint of financial return.
  The project's uniqueness is true, so it needs no exaggeration.
- **Fast, then dazzling.** The work is the first paint; nothing astonishing may delay it.
