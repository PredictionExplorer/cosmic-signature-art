What is real here

The orbit is computed in your browser from the published seed by a line-for-line port of the generator's own stream and integrator (Yoshida, fourth order, dt 0.001). It agrees with the published record to the last bit. The drawing is the orbit in the plane of its greatest extent, not the artwork.

The crossing shows only pixels of the two published stills (1920-pixel rungs cut from the 16-bit masters). The order comes from the films: for Light, when each pixel first reached half its final brightness; for Ink, when it stopped changing. Analysis in docs; nothing is invented between the two.

All the light adds the 1280-pixel Light rungs of all works in linear light and compresses the total with a soft shoulder. It is a derived index and says so. A work chosen alone is shown as its own untouched still.
